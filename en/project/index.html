Home /The Project

A condominium built for industry.

Independent titled lots, finished common areas and industrial zoning in one of the fastest-growing logistics areas of the Central Valley.

Concept

Your own lot, with shared services.

Cubo Park is set up under Costa Rica’s condominium property regime. Each lot has its own cadastral survey and title, so every unit can be bought, financed or sold on its own.

Lots are designated for industrial, warehouse, flex-space and office use. The condominium handles the access road, wastewater treatment, stormwater retention and green areas in common.

Infrastructure

What is already built

Internal road

Paved vehicle access with curbs running the full length of the condominium, from the public street to the last lot.

Treatment plant

Shared wastewater treatment plant at the north end of the property.

Retention pond

Lined stormwater retention pond that regulates runoff from the condominium.

Green areas

Green areas between lots and a protected buffer along the Bermúdez River.

Parking

Parking and maneuvering areas in front of the existing warehouse.

Cadastral surveys

Every lot has its own cadastral survey registered with the National Registry.
