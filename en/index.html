The condominium

Infrastructure in place. Your company is next.

Cubo Park is an industrial and warehouse condominium made up of independent lots. Each lot has its own cadastral survey and is sold with its own title, backed by common areas that are already built.

  • Paved internal road with vehicle access
  • Treatment plant and retention pond
  • Lots suitable for industry, warehouses and office-warehouses
  • Green areas and Bermúdez River buffer
