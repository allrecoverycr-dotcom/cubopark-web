Overview

Lot FFM-IB-4 sits at the condominium entrance, fronting the public street. It includes an existing house with mature gardens and trees. Like the other lots, it is suitable for industry, warehouses and office-warehouses, with the added benefit of public street frontage. The land is priced at $350 per m², plus the value of the house.
