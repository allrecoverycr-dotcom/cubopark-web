Overview

Cubo Park’s warehouse sits on lot FFM-IB-5, at the center of the condominium. The main hall offers a wide, open floor surrounded by two levels of steel mezzanines with glass-railed walkways linking the office areas.

It includes glass-enclosed offices, a reception, service areas, roll-up vehicle doors, natural ventilation through louvers and roof skylights. It suits a distribution center, light manufacturing, a showroom or a corporate headquarters with operations.
